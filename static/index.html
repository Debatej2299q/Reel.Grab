How it works

  1. Copy the link. In Instagram, open the reel or post, tap the share icon and choose "Copy link".
  2. Paste it above. We fetch the video details so you can check it's the right one.
  3. Pick a quality and download. The file is saved as an MP4 with sound, or as an MP3 if you only want the audio.

Questions

Does it work with private accounts?

No. Only public posts can be downloaded. If an account is private, Instagram doesn't let anyone without access see the video, and neither can this tool.

Why did my download say Instagram refused the request?

Instagram occasionally blocks automated requests or asks for a login. Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, the post may have been removed.

Is it legal to download videos?

Save only videos you made or have permission to use. Videos belong to their creators, so don't re-upload someone else's work as your own.

Do you keep the videos?

No. The file is prepared for your download and deleted from the server right after it is sent.
